# Privacy Policy – Tiny Theater

**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

Tiny Theater is developed by TableForOne and published by TableForAll.

Tiny Theater does not collect personal information through its own servers and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

### Data Collection

Tiny Theater does not directly collect, store, or share personal information.

The game uses Steam features such as achievements, leaderboards, and Steam Cloud. These features may process certain information through Steam, including your Steam ID, username, scores, achievements, and save data.

We do not operate our own servers or maintain a separate database of player information.

### Steam Services

Tiny Theater uses the following Steam features:

- **Leaderboards:** Scores are submitted to Steam and may be visible to other players along with your Steam username.
- **Achievements:** Unlocked achievements are recorded through Steam.
- **Steam Cloud:** Save files may be stored and synchronized through Steam if enabled.

These services are provided by Valve Corporation and are subject to Steam's Privacy Policy:

https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

### Local Game Data

Tiny Theater does save game progress and settings locally on your computer.

If Steam Cloud is enabled, these files may also be synchronized through Steam. We do not upload or store your save files on our own servers.

### Third-Party Services

Apart from Steam, Tiny Theater does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

We do not sell player information or use it for marketing.

### Your Data

You can manage your Steam account information and privacy settings through Steam.

For information about how Valve handles your data, please refer to Steam's Privacy Policy.

If you have any privacy-related questions about Tiny Theater, you can contact us directly.

### Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected by the date at the top of this page.
